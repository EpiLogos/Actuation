#0 · Why?

Make agency understandable

Actuation addresses a gap between asking AI to work and understanding the agency that actually acts. A person needs to know who is acting, in which World, with what purpose and permission, and how the experience of that work returns. As work moves between models, harnesses, sessions and collaborating Agents, these relationships should stay inspectable. Actuation gives them an explicit form so that authority can remain answerable to consequence.

#1 · What?

A CLI and portable contracts for agency

Actuation is a CLI and a set of portable software contracts for identifying, inspecting and studying technological agency. Its current product detects available harnesses and reads structured accounts of situated Agency, realised acting loops, their unfolding Streams, semantic Activity and model-bearing instantiation. Its wider purpose is to make the constitution and management of agency an explicit part of an O:I World, from an ordinary coding session to a bounded composition of Agents.

Read the expanded #1 — What?

#2 · How?

Connect the actor, its body and what happens

Actuation relates an enduring Agent to a situated Agency and an explicit WorldBinding. Receipts identify the acting condition and its model, harness and material relations. Ordered Streams preserve attributable events; Activity makes those events understandable; Return carries results, evidence and difference back to the governing relation. Portable contracts let native runtimes supply these facts while preserving their own richer records.

#3 · Who / Whereby?

For people who work with and build agency

Actuation serves people shaping their relationship with AI, Agents working within declared bounds, and developers connecting harnesses and interfaces. Its identity and authority contracts make collaboration inspectable: an existing Agent can receive delegated work, a new Agent can have explicit lineage, and an independent participant can retain its own standing. Researchers can then study how the constitution of agency changes the work it produces.

#4 · Where / When?

Where a model is actually acting

Actuation begins wherever a model acts in a persistent working World, including an ordinary directory-bound coding harness. The same semantic account can accompany a richer body, another Surface or a different machine. Observations belong to their actual time and environment. Continuity and change remain attributable as sessions, models and material conditions evolve.

#5 · Why-For?

Agency that can receive what its work reveals

Actuation is for an O:I World in which agency can be deliberately constituted, encountered and revised. Central supplies durable authored ground; AIKit provisions the operative body and context; Workcell supplies material execution; Factory commissions developmental work; O:I presents the whole. Actuation gives that work an intelligible actor and a return path. Its research programme investigates how models, contexts and ways of acting can be cultivated, while keeping experiment results distinct from the intentions that motivate them.

#0Why?

Make agency understandable

Why? — the expanded product account.

Return to the 0/1 seedSource-grounded candidate · review draft
vision

The missing account of who is acting

A coding session can edit a project before anyone has clearly expressed the relationship between its Agent, the person, the project and the machine. The immediate work may succeed. Later, the person still needs to understand whose purpose guided it, which bounds applied and what survived the session.

Actuation makes this relationship an object that can be inspected. The experiential aim is simple: when a person asks for work, they can locate the actor, understand the commission and follow what comes back. An Agent joining existing work can discover its situated role without treating its process identifier or model name as its identity.

The [[source:constitution|constitution]] establishes this intention. The [[source:wayfinder|wayfinder]] develops it through actual harnesses and research. Its later correction makes the ordinary acting loop the starting case. This keeps the product grounded in AI work people already do.

Capabilities: [[cap.actuation.realised|Realised]].

vision

Let experience reach the governing decision

Someone determines the purpose and bounds of work; an actor encounters the actual world. Those two positions may belong to different Agents or to the same Agent at different moments. The encounter can expose a false assumption, a missing capability, a useful artifact or a disagreement.

The governing relation needs to receive that difference before deciding what follows. A useful return preserves who found it, what supports it and which work produced it. The person can then revise a commission, recognise a result or retain a disagreement for further investigation.

This is why Return belongs near Actuation’s purpose. It gives the agency that determines work a way to learn from its consequences. A single polished answer is often useful, but the underlying evidence and attributable contributions are also needed when the answer is questioned.

Capabilities: [[cap.actuation.return|Return]].

founding position

Ground the product in shared identities

An Agent is an enduring identity. Agency is that Agent situated for an act, with a World, role, capability, authority and context. A session supplies runtime continuity. Execution is a concrete act. A harness supplies an operational body.

These meanings are grounded in the [[source:covenant|O:I Covenant]] and [[source:foundations|founding positions]], with the earlier [[source:primitives|experienced ontology]] retained as provenance. Actuation’s native contracts make the agency relations concrete. The distinctions help answer everyday questions: did an existing Agent change its working body, or was a new Agent deliberately created?

The six questions in this account organise a candidate product understanding. Their use follows the [[source:ql|QL foundation]] and the human-selected documentation convention. Formal coordinates can help inspect a relation while the public agency contracts retain semantic field names.

readme

What you can use from the checkout

The usable package is a Node-based CLI with versioned contract modules. A terminal user can list commands and contracts, inspect a supplied JSON agency document, read an acting-loop receipt, inspect a Stream or Activity, and discover locally available harnesses. Applications can call the exported modules directly to validate or construct the same portable readings.

The current command table also exposes an instantiation recorder. It relates a supplied model-bearing receipt to a fresh harness detection and can append the bound result as JSONL. This gives an integration a concrete record of what it claimed to instantiate and which local detection supported that claim.

Run node bin/actuation capabilities --json from the checkout to inspect its command surface. The [[source:commands|command table]] is the inspected implementation. Several CLI and detection files already contain unrelated working-tree changes at this documentation snapshot; the account records those as inspected checkout behaviour, rather than silently attributing them to committed main.

Capabilities: [[cap.actuation.discovery|Discovery]] · [[cap.actuation.instantiation|Instantiation]].

surface discovery

Inspect situated authority and lineage

The agency contract reads a WorldBinding together with root scope, metagency grants, determinations and Returns. The result identifies the Agent, Agency, World and scope, and states which explicit metagency operations are available in that relation.

Root Agency means the Agency bound to the enclosing World for the scope being read. The role is positional. A person can therefore inspect a project’s governing Agency using the same grammar as an Agency governing a wider World.

Determination records preserve self-differentiation, delegation, derivation and federation. Recursive lineage checks connect a child determination to its exact parent and granted autonomy. These are implemented validation and reading facilities. The separate public operation that actually creates or binds Agency remains a development question in the latest recovered wayfinder correction.

Capabilities: [[cap.actuation.world-binding|World Binding]] · [[cap.actuation.metagency|Metagency]] · [[cap.actuation.determination|Determination]].

surface discovery

Inspect acting and follow its unfolding

A realised Actuation receipt accounts for an existing acting condition. It binds Agent, Agency, WorldBinding and Actuation to evidence of recurrence. A minimal valid case can describe a model acting in a persistent directory-bound World. Richer receipts add external harness, session, process, model and material references.

A Stream supplies ordered attributable material from that act: messages, tool operations, observations, artifacts, interruptions and Returns. A cursor lets a reader resume after a known event sequence. The reference Journal demonstrates append, replay, subscription and closure using the portable contract.

Activity gives a consumer a semantic reading of that material. It can identify the subject, phase and outcome, and expose the trace relationship needed to inspect the underlying events. A meaningful world observation can be Activity even when no Action invocation produced it.

Capabilities: [[cap.actuation.realised|Realised]] · [[cap.actuation.stream|Stream]] · [[cap.actuation.activity|Activity]].

implementation guide

Understand the session already in front of you

Begin with the ordinary situation: a person has opened a coding harness inside a project and asked it to work. The integration should disclose the existing Agent and situated Agency, the World being acted within and evidence that the loop is actually acting. A supplied realised receipt can then be read through actuation realised receipt.json --json.

The useful result is an inspectable account of the present actor. If the harness can disclose a session, process or material binding, those references enrich the account. If a facility is unsupported, the receipt carries that condition. An interface can show the basic acting relationship first and reveal the richer body facts on request.

Harness detection is a complementary step. actuation harness catalog --json describes detectable candidates; actuation harness detect --json inspects this machine. Installation evidence and an observed acting receipt answer different questions. The encounter should make both easy to inspect without making a person interpret a raw process listing.

Capabilities: [[cap.actuation.harness-detection|Harness Detection]] · [[cap.actuation.realised|Realised]].

constitution design commitment

Give another Agent bounded work

The intended journey begins with a human or governing Agency selecting an existing Agent, or explicitly determining a new one where authorised. The commission identifies the World, purpose, permitted autonomy, bounds and Return relation. If an independent Agent joins, the relationship preserves its independent origin and the authority it actually granted.

Before a nested commission is accepted, its parent relation should be inspectable. The recipient needs to understand what it can decide locally and which questions must return. If it meets a constraint or evidence that changes the task, it should return that material with attribution so the governing decision can be revised.

The agency module currently validates these relations and produces a read model. The September 2 wayfinder comment asks for the smallest public create/bind operation and its conformance. The inspected CLI does not expose that mutation. This journey is consequently a design commitment supported by executable relation checks, with the public commissioning step still requiring native implementation or discovery and proof.

Capabilities: [[cap.actuation.determination|Determination]] · [[cap.actuation.metagency|Metagency]] · [[cap.actuation.return|Return]].

protocol method

Follow work, inspect evidence and receive Return

A reader first sees an Activity summary: what is happening, the subject and the current result or phase. Selecting its trace opens the corresponding Stream and event references. Sequence cursors let another Surface resume the same material after a disconnect, provided its provider implements that continuity.

As several loci contribute, event attribution preserves the contribution of each. When a Return arrives, it cites a Return reference and can connect to artifacts and evidence. A review can therefore follow the result back to the work that produced it and retain dissent before synthesis.

Receipt, recognition and a resulting World change are separate steps. The native Return validator enforces their ordering. The intended interface should make those steps visible: received material awaits a decision; a recognised result may authorise a later change; an applied change should have its own evidence. Factory and source-owning products carry their respective recognition and mutation procedures.

Capabilities: [[cap.actuation.stream|Stream]] · [[cap.actuation.activity|Activity]] · [[cap.actuation.return|Return]].

architecture contract

The relationships that make one actor intelligible

The portable design uses stable references to connect identities whose owners can evolve independently. WorldBinding supplies the situated relation between an Agency and its World. A realised receipt relates that semantic actor to the actual body condition. The Stream relates its unfolding material to the same Actuation and Agency.

Body replacement is a useful test of this design. If an Agent reconnects through another process or model condition, an observer should identify the change while retaining the enduring Agent where that continuity is actually declared. The exported continuityDelta compares realised receipts for this purpose.

This makes the architecture useful to the person who changes machine, harness or Surface. They can ask what changed and what continued. The answer comes from explicit references and observations; runtime names alone cannot settle semantic continuity.

Capabilities: [[cap.actuation.continuity|Continuity]] · [[cap.actuation.realised|Realised]].

architecture contract

Authority is an explicit relation

A WorldBinding carries bounds, authority and references to constraints such as human commitments, security policy, evidence and external reality. Metagency grants specify operations over agency within that binding. A root position makes scope intelligible while those constraints remain in force.

The native determination checks preserve a practical boundary: communication or participation alone does not establish authority over another Agent. Federation can relate independent participants. Delegation can carry explicit bounded authority. Recursion requires the parent’s granted autonomy and exact lineage.

These checks establish the validity of supplied portable relations. Runtime enforcement, launch, source writes and material permissions still require their native operations. An interface must distinguish reading a valid grant from exercising it. This is the bounded contract on which the intended Create Agent or commission experience should build.

Capabilities: [[cap.actuation.world-binding|World Binding]] · [[cap.actuation.metagency|Metagency]] · [[cap.actuation.determination|Determination]].

architecture contract

Each product contributes its native part

Central provides durable authored ground and the project or personal World in which agency is situated. AIKit resolves the body, Context, Skills, models, sessions and Surfaces for an operative locus. Workcell materialises processes, services, storage and lifecycle. Actuation supplies the semantic relationship of agency and its attributable acting.

Factory uses that agency when developmental work needs a commission, evidence and a candidate result. O:I can present the relationships through its desktop and communication Surfaces. Activity’s attention signal supplies information to that whole; the consuming product determines notification and attention policy.

The integration direction is concrete: preserve the supplied Agency and Actuation references when provisioning a body, project its Stream rather than inventing a disconnected activity history, and return native evidence by reference. The [[source:placement|placement account]] and [[source:loop|realised-loop contract]] explain these owner boundaries.

protocol method

Use observations in their actual environment

Harness discovery describes this machine at the moment the probes run. Catalog descriptors declare the supported probe strategies. Results distinguish detected, absent and unavailable conditions, with the evidence or reason needed to interpret them. Optional version probing executes a declared version command, so callers should choose it deliberately when richer receipts are needed.

Self-detection reads known environment markers and compares them with the local detection result. Nested harnesses can leave several markers. An ambiguous result is useful information: the caller should retain the candidates rather than guessing which is innermost.

A recorded instantiation can attach detection evidence to a harness binding and append JSONL to a chosen file. That operation supplies local attribution. It does not itself establish the quality of a model’s work or prove that every declared remote facility is available. The person or integration can follow the recorded references when a later environment behaves differently.

Capabilities: [[cap.actuation.harness-detection|Harness Detection]] · [[cap.actuation.harness-self|Harness Self]] · [[cap.actuation.instantiation|Instantiation]].

research notes transcripts

Study how the constitution changes the work

Actuation also houses experiments on model-bearing agency, harnesses and epistemic cultivation: the deliberate shaping of source material, contexts, dialogue and learning conditions. The wayfinder asks how those choices change provenance handling, uncertainty, contradiction retention, transfer and synthesis.

The QL runtime programme supplies pinned experimental bodies and comparison procedures. Its migration record preserves the historical evidence boundary: structural conformance existed while live Series 1 capability effects were unclaimed at migration. The Prime programme continues recursive relational Agency and harness research as an open experimental direction.

A useful experiment varies an explicit condition, retains attributable inputs and outputs, and returns a finding that can survive critique or reproduction. This account does not infer a positive QL effect from structural tests. The research belongs in the product’s purpose and horizon, while each result remains attached to its actual experiment and evidence.

The smallest unresolved product step is the public determination journey identified by the corrected wayfinder: bind an existing Agent into a project World, or explicitly actualise a permitted new Agency, retaining lineage, bounds and Return. Why-For? — the expanded product account.

vision

An intelligible actor across the whole World

The intended outcome is a World where a person can encounter agency without reconstructing it from scattered logs. They can inspect who acts, follow the work through its Surfaces, understand the relevant authority and receive the evidence that should shape what happens next.

The minimal case is already familiar: an Agent working in a project directory. The fuller case can involve several Agents, models and machines. The account stays coherent because identity, situated role, body, activity and Return have explicit relationships at both scales.

For the suite, this supplies the actor-related meaning that other products can compose. Central’s ground can inform the act; AIKit can provision it; Workcell can realise its body; Factory can develop through it; O:I can make it encounterable. The resulting work can then return to the World that bore its consequences.
