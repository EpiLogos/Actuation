# World-bound Agency, positional Root Agency and Return

This document accompanies `contracts/agency.mjs` and `contracts/agency-v1.schema.json`. The executable contract is normative where prose and code differ.

## Constitutional relation

`Agent` remains enduring identity. `Agency` is the situated capacity of that Agent to act. `WorldBinding` says which operative world and scope that Agency inhabits, under which bounds, authority and external constraints.

Root Agency is not a class. `isRootAgency(binding, scope)` is true only when the ordinary Agency's bound world is the enclosing Objective Internality for the scope being read.

The same Agency can therefore be governed upward and governing downward. Recursive `Determination` preserves the exact parent relation and requires the parent-granted delegated autonomy before a differentiated Agency can determine another Agency.

`MetagencyGrant` is explicit authority to determine/configure/actualise/reintegrate agency in a bound world. Visibility of another Agent, communication with it, or A2A reachability is not metagency.

## Distinct multi-agent relations

`Determination.kind` keeps these separate:

- `self-differentiation` — one Agency articulates a bounded differentiated locus of itself;
- `delegation` — authority is explicitly delegated to another existing Agency;
- `derivation` — a new Agent/Agency continuity is actualised with explicit lineage;
- `federation` — independently grounded Agencies cooperate without inherited determining authority.

Federation carrying determining authority is rejected by the executable validator; use an explicit delegation relation instead.

## Return is not synthesis

A `Return` carries difference, artifacts/claims/evidence and Agency/material/external provenance before any synthesis. Its lifecycle has three independent facts:

1. `received` — returned material has reached the governing relation;
2. `recognition_state` — that material has been recognised/rejected or remains pending;
3. `world_mutation_state` — the governing world has actually been changed.

The validator prevents world mutation from being represented as applied before explicit recognition.

## Constraints above technical Root Agency

`WorldBinding.constraints` retains refs to human-authored commitments, security policy, evidence and external reality. Root position within a technical Objective Internality therefore does not imply sovereignty over the human, source authority, external Others or the world.

Central may provide residence/persistent authored ground; AIKit may provide the resolved operational body/session/Surfaces; Workcell may provide material execution; Factory may commission developmental agency. None of those implementation identities define `WorldBinding` or Root Agency.
