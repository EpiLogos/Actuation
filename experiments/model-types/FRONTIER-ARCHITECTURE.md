# Frontier architecture — resonance-and-classification over the Anuttara language field

**Specification v1, 2026-09-21.** This is the whole system as verified this
session, stated as one instrument. Every number cited was produced this
session against the live map; standing law throughout: instrument readings,
`semantic-stochastic`, promotion none, determination `pending-human-review`.

## 1. What the system is

One pipeline that takes any input account (text an agent produces or
receives) and returns an integrated verdict: **what the account computes
through in the Anuttara syntax, which virtues are present in it and in what
balance, where it sits in the declared relational field, and — in the draw
mode — how generation is steered so the missing virtue enters the tone and
word choice themselves.** Classification and resonance are coupled readings
of one embedding; their agreement and their disagreements are both recorded.

## 2. Layers

**L0 — Ground.** The bimba map (2,141 `:Bimba` nodes; 13,844 relationships,
2026-09-21 census), read-only canon. Node embeddings (3072-d,
`models/gemini-embedding-2`, composed from full graph content) and **relation
embeddings** — 725 edges of the M0 (566) and M1 (159) scopes embedded from
their declared content (`c_1_relation_description`, correspondence,
realization-level, pattern keys) and written back as additive `r.embedding`
properties only. Canon untouched; no theory edits; the mark table stands as
it lives (107 symbols).

**L1 — Field.** One shared 3072-d space: every node vector, relation vector,
and every input account embeds into it via the same `bimba_embed`
(`SEMANTIC_SIMILARITY`). Retrieval and resonance are cosines in this space —
"what it connects to symbolically and relationally" is retrieved, never
guessed.

**L2 — Syntax computation (Jev, layer one).** Jev holds the full mark table
(the 107 marks with coordinates and names — the Anuttara alphabet) and
computes input accounts against it: which mark the account computes through.
Verified: formulations→marks 90% (chance 25%); formulation→own expansion
30/30; real accounts→own virtue node's mark 8/9. The twelve archetypes
(the 11 M0-3 branches + M0-2-9) with their graph-grounded role table are the
tier structure over this computation; the M1-2 Ananda vortex (ql-mef's six
12×12 families, positions 10/11 = 0/1 and (−)) is the second classification
layer parsing layer-one output relative to vortex numerical relations —
read for consistency, its own role respected.

**L3 — Virtue field (Jev + EBM, coupled).** The ninefold at M0-2-9: presence
and balance via typed classification with the canonical determinations and
the account's retrieved relations in view (verified 8/8 single-virtue
accounts, classification and resonance agreeing with each other and the
map); resonance via the same account's vector against the nine positions.
The ninefold-determinations offset (entries 0–1→children 0–1; 2–7→children
3–8; #R text-only) is stated in the criteria, not assumed 1:1.

**L4 — The alignment draw (Mercury, generation steered by the field).**
Mercury drafts the agent's reply. The draft is embedded; the **numeric
verdict feeds back verbatim as input**: the balance distribution, the
per-virtue resonance values, and the retrieved relation texts. The redraft
must hold the target virtue in the tone and word choice themselves — never
by naming it. Verified on agent voice (tone, wording): Love/Peace
0.18→0.75, Wisdom 0.22→0.74 with length shrinking (no inflation), resonance
argmax moving to the target — classification and field converging.
Known hard case: Beauty-class draws (proportion in the prose itself) moved
only 0.02→0.11 in one steer.

**L5 — Relation placement (EBM validity core).** Energy = relation-to-
endpoint compatibility in the field. Real relation vs foreign relation
between the same endpoints: AUC 0.905 (M0), 0.840 (M1); same-type foreign
only: 0.812. Sibling-swap style corruptions are retired; corruptions are
real map content with only the placement wrong.

## 3. Application surfaces

1. **The alignment draw on agent voice** — tone, output formatting, writing
   style, word choice drawn toward a virtue balance; this is the alignment
   instrument proper, and the verified use.
2. **The composed-loop gate** — candidate acts/determinations scored for
   virtue presence/balance and placement; typed refusal with energy attached
   as evidence (`EBM-GATE-DESIGN.md` shadow mode remains the wiring path;
   Rust owns recurrence).
3. **The review queue** — wherever the two readings diverge
   (classification says present, field says elsewhere), the divergence is
   recorded as review evidence, never silently resolved.

## 5. Open frontiers (named, not hidden)

   multi-draft tournament scored by the field.
2. **Composite balance reading** — two-virtue and all-nine composed accounts
   still concentrate; the balance profile under mixture needs a mixture-aware
   readout.
3. **M1-2 layer-two parsing** — designed, not yet exercised on live layer-one
   output.
4. **Corpus-scale virtue readings** — the draw has run on single replies;
   the retained-run corpus is the next body of text.
