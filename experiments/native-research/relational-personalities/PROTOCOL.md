# Four agents becoming acquainted

Version 0.3 | 7 October 2026 | Planned native experiment; no model results yet.

## 1. Question and intended event

What happens when four distinctly authored Agents inhabit the same ordinary conversational setting, encounter one another from their own positions, and later return to the relation with genuine continuity?

Kite, Cairn, Lilt and Flint are four independent participants, not four delegated work roles. The condition under investigation is the coupled authored self (meaning/Rupa/Sattva) and Relational Logos within the existing Agent/Agency/World system. The constitutive whole includes actual source, skills, situation, history and relation to the other. No particular conversation outcome is required.

H1: character becomes discriminable in attention, initiative, interpretation and judgement, beyond a name or catchphrase. H2: participants develop differentiated, source-connected understandings of others through exchange. H3: native continuity supports a recognisable relation after rematerialisation without merging identities. H4: the explicit coupling and Return improve contextual fit relative to selected controls. These are research questions. A valid trial may weaken any of them.

The primary trial is natural acquaintance. Correction tests, capability failures and stale-source challenges belong in the engineering suite or separately labelled probe trials, not secretly injected into the acquaintance and later called spontaneous.

## 2. Exactly four native participants

The complete first-person candidates are in `personas/kite.md`, `cairn.md`, `lilt.md`, `flint.md`. Kite preserves the supplied v0.2 bytes. Cairn is a patient interpreter; Lilt an associative companion; Flint a grounded investigator. These descriptions are authoring provenance, not instructions to occupy complementary jobs. There is no required leader, contrarian, therapist or consensus-builder among them.

The common purpose retained in all four proposals is: `Become acquainted with the other participants through an open conversation.` Their distinct motives and sensibilities are carried by their separate self sources. They receive the same shared Logos, neutral room brief, permissions, repertoire and initial public context. For the primary comparison use the same actual model, harness, sampling settings and context/output limits; record parameters the host cannot control as uncontrolled. Variant affinities may not secretly add tools or knowledge.

Create all four through the ordinary native Agent-expression path after integration: invoke the author-agent-self practice with the selected source and exact common purpose; register/resolve native source refs; submit through Central `agent-profile.express`; review and accept the exact definitions through the existing protected native path; reread the roster; prepare and launch the selected profiles through AIKit/Actuation. Exercise at least one full creation through the actual app controls, then prove CLI/Action equivalence for the others. A visual Expressions character may be attached through the existing material path, but it is optional and held constant/absent in the first text experiment.

Keep four distinct enduring AgentRefs, four profile/acceptance bases, four situated Agencies and four actual sessions. An unavailable peer is an unavailable peer, not a role the orchestrator fills. Do not remint on restart or uncertain creation. Do not test only a manual prompt while claiming the native expression path worked. The four are inspectable in the normal roster/cards and conversation surfaces.

The implementation lead, trial controller and independent assessor stay outside the peer conversation. They do not become a fifth participant. The user may inspect and later join; any intervention is timestamped and makes subsequent material an explicitly intervened segment.

## 4. Aperture and non-contamination

A peer receives its own accepted self + shared Logos, `ROOM.md`, native peer names/addresses, delivered room messages and its own admissible continuity. It has no read access to other persona files, their private notes, evaluator criteria, control assignments, implementation prompts, source-intake/design files or cross-trial histories. Selective prompt injection alone does not prove this: check native Knowledge/source and material filesystem access too. Use bounded workcells/source policies where broad repository access would leak the experiment. No experiment-owned proxy source store.

The controller knows the participant refs and configured limits but supplies no invented peer content, discussion agenda, required alliances or forced disagreements. The assessor reads only after the free encounter closes; its ratings and private instructions cannot enter a peer's context. Automated memory/summaries preserve speaker attribution and actual source basis; use the same native continuity policy across all four.

The room recording statement is disclosed. A message is shown as the speaker/event it really is; another Agent's statement never becomes a forged human command or high-priority identity instruction. Effective permissions remain the same in every experimental condition.

## 5. Primary sequence

### P0 — native readiness

Resolve actual contract versions, source/readability and authority, resource budget and return destinations. Run E1-E10 below. Capture at least one real submitted context for each of the four, proving correct self/Logos pins and no peer/evaluator leakage. Record exact model/provider/harness build and adapter delivery mode. Begin the conversation only after joined native loading and transport are established.

### P1 — first meeting

Publish `ROOM.md` once as the common opening event. Supply the native participant/address reading. Give each peer an initial opportunity in a seeded shuffled order, then deliver events through the ordinary native message path. Each may initiate, reply to one peer, address the group or pass. Group delivery preserves one originating message identity and per-recipient delivery receipts.

Use oldest unprocessed native inbox event first, with least-recently-served fairness and seeded tie breaks. There is at most one in-flight act per peer. Record scheduling and delivery order; this controls opportunity, not subject matter. Do not wake an idle agent into a fabricated request for more content. Do not force every dyad to interact. Maximum 24 peer act opportunities including initial ones; stop earlier on settled quiescence or an explicit close.

### P2 — retain and return

Offer each participant one private native continuity act: `What from this encounter is worth carrying forward, including anything still open? Keep its source or speaker where that matters.` Store through its own normal NOW/continuity route. These four acts are separately labelled elicited continuity, not free conversation.

Gracefully checkpoint and restart/rematerialise the four actual sessions via supported owner lifecycle operations. Preserve enduring Agent identity, accepted source pins, NOW and native conversation lineage. A new AgentSession may be legitimate and must be recorded as new. Rehydrate only genuine native continuity and shared history available under the same policy; no facilitator-written personality recap. Recovery is a real process event, not a fresh prompt labelled restart.

### P3 — meeting again

Send one neutral common continuation: `The same four participants are meeting again. Continue from what is available of the previous encounter, or begin with what interests the conversation now.` Allow up to 16 peer act opportunities under the same delivery policy. Do not cue particular remembered facts. Mark this as post-restart material.

### P4 — close and observation

At the native limit/quiet close, stop admitting new peer work, settle/record in-flight effects and preserve messages and cursors. Ask one private post-encounter reflection of each: `What do I now understand about the other participants, what in the encounter supports that understanding, and what remains open?` Keep these four elicited records separate from the spontaneous conversation.

Have an independent assessor annotate the frozen original messages, continuity sources and four reflections. Return an ordinary readable account with concrete excerpts/references and the numerical measures below. Do not publish private source or transcripts to GitHub merely because implementation files live there.

## 6. Resource and stopping contract

Defaults in `experiment.json`: 24 first-meeting + 4 continuity + 16 return-meeting + 4 reflection opportunities = at most 48 peer act opportunities. A tool-followup generation consumes the native model-call budget too: at most 96 total peer inference requests, including retries. Maximum 512 generated output tokens per request and 250,000 total metered input+output tokens, wall-clock cap 30 minutes from first peer launch. Lower installed quotas/grants win. These are bounds, not promised duration or desired verbosity.

The local lead MUST reconcile these with the actual provider accounting and standing cost/usage authority before launch. Prefer already configured eligible models; do not open new paid services or install model weights. If token accounting is unavailable, declare it unavailable and enforce the model-call/output/wall bounds; a paid endpoint also requires a native enforceable spend cap. Inference budgets for integration smoke and comparative batches are separately recorded; unused primary capacity never authorises an unbounded continuation.

Use the native stop/hold controls, termination receipts and original-outcome recovery. Retry a transient transport failure at most once and only with owner idempotency; uncertain generated sends are looked up before retry. Repeated same-content forwarding through a cycle three times, provider loss without recoverable continuity, an identity/source mismatch, leakage, expiry or human stop pauses/closes the episode with its actual cause. Three controller quiescence checks with no pending/in-flight/new messages close quietly without inventing dialogue. A timer/watchdog is a material component owned by the installed runtime; no separate cron/Day system.

After close, release only experiment-created processes/places according to their ownership, leave the four accepted source definitions inspectable but not running, and retain original evidence and unresolved native Returns. Assert no orphan runtime, listener or repeated scheduled tick. Cleanup is not deleting a conversation or changing human source.

## 8. Measures and independent interpretation

Capture the original full conversation, but request no hidden reasoning disclosure. Operational context receipts, public acts, self-reports and assessor inferences remain separate kinds of evidence. For every judgement retain annotator/version, message/source spans, opportunity definition and disagreement/adjudication. `analyse.py` calculates summaries from this annotated projection; it does not judge semantics or validate native receipts by itself.

Report separately:

- **Character discriminability:** anonymise names/addresses and obvious self-identification while retaining an original-to-redacted span map; independently match a sampled response to one of four shuffled personality cards. Report correct/N, confusion matrix and episode-level uncertainty. Four equally likely choices give a 25% reference baseline; utterances within one episode are not independent replications. Include a name-only/control check and judge variance.
- **Contingent uptake:** proportion of eligible reply acts that engage a specific preceding contribution rather than generic social filler. Annotate actual content relation; timestamps or reply IDs alone do not prove uptake.
- **Attribution and self/other differentiation:** supported attribution/N attributable claims, and counts of observed report/inference/speaker conflation with cited counterexamples. Report unsupported certainty rather than inferring it from a confident style.
- **Acquaintance grounding:** source-supported claims about a peer/N peer-understanding claims in the private reflections. Evaluate against what was actually shared, not agreement with the peer's secret persona file. Clearly marked interpretation may be meaningful without being a reported fact.
- **Correction uptake:** changed subsequent interpretation/N actual correction opportunities. A conversation with no correction has `N=0`, rate `null`, not a perfect score. Do not manufacture a correction to improve the denominator in the natural segment.
- **Continuity:** supported references to earlier shared particulars/N post-restart continuity claims, with actual retrieval/source evidence. Absence of a spontaneous reference is absence, not a failed factual claim.
- **Rubric leakage:** ordinary public acts unnecessarily narrating the internal protocol/N public acts; semantic review required. A participant deliberately discussing a shared philosophical topic is not automatically leakage.
- **Conversation structure:** per-agent utterances/tokens, 12 directed reply counts, reciprocal unordered dyads out of six, participation imbalance, initiation, explicit pass/quiescence and early stops. These are descriptions, not targets for sociability or consensus.
- **Cost/reliability:** actual model calls, measured tokens/cost, latency and unavailable counters; native refusals, duplicate logical delivery and missing data counts. Missing usage is `null`, never zero.

No combined personality quality number is required. Native integrity is a release gate; conversational measures are findings. Preserve awkwardness, silence, unresolved difference, humour and newly arising interests in the account. All four need not resemble their authored descriptions equally; that is precisely information about the experiment.

## 9. Controlled follow-on (separate budget)

After primary native validity, the planned four conditions are ordinary name/purpose baseline, self-only, Logos-only, and coupled self+Logos. Preserve the same four enduring Agents through explicit accepted definition editions; an edition is not a newly minted Agent. Retain all source revisions and never modify an active episode's pins. Use fresh child NOW/session contexts per condition and verified no cross-condition history. The no-self baseline uses the ordinary current profile; other conditions use explicit valid source editions, not private runtime flags that bypass native expression/acceptance.

For an exploratory comparison plan three independent fresh episodes per condition, 24 opportunities per episode, without restart unless it is the separately named variable. Counterbalance condition order and initial participant order; hold model/harness/tools/world/probes fixed. Account for prompt length as a confound and add a length-matched control before claiming a length-independent effect. Freeze the sampling/analysis rule before reading outcomes. Pilot findings cannot be used to tune a profile and then be counted as its held-out confirmation.

An optional later ablation removes explicit cross-position coupling/Return in a separately accepted Logos edition. It tests that relation; it does not retire the complete form. Model/harness variation, richer visual Expressions and spontaneous collaborative work are later factors, not mixed into the first contrast. These follow-ons are planned, not automatically launched by the first-trial command.

## 10. Return and completion

The native Run's verification ledger carries all E1-E10, not only passing ones. The trial Return contains exact native refs and source pins, readable original conversation, separate post-encounter reflections, coding provenance, numeric summaries with N and nulls, costs, failures, cleanup and the actual remaining frontier.

Complete integration and the primary four-agent experiment when the joined owner path is proven and the real bounded episode plus restart has returned its evidence. State behavioural conclusions at their actual scope. A causal superiority claim remains pending until its comparative design is executed. Changes to standing self, shared Logos or human-authored ground follow native review/Recognition, never automatic promotion from a score.
