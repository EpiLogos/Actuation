# Namespaced Deep QL research — #108

This surface is deliberately non-blocking. `ql.harmonic`, `ql.state64`, `ql.mef`, `ql.context`, `ql.epogdoon`, and `ql.topology` are research namespaces only.

No 36/64 harmonic field, automatic MEF operation, context-frame deepening, literal 9/8 metric, or higher topology is promoted into required runtime control here. Promotion must satisfy the specification's three tests: semantic necessity, a precise computational definition, and demonstrated experimental/architectural value, followed by a versioned specification change.
