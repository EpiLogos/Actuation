Relational contract: the P positions P0–P5 and their conjugates P0′–P5′ are purely relational responsibilities in the recurrence of work; they hold no inherent manifest content. The twelve MEF lenses are the refraction media of these positions: whatever content a position carries is read through a lens, and a lens reading is always of a position's responsibility — never of the thing itself. Readings inform; the work remains yours.
