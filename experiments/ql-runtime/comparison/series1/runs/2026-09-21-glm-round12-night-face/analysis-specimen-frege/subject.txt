Sense and reference — Frege (1892), Über Sinn und Bedeutung

> "The morning star and the evening star are the same body — and yet the
> thought expressed by 'the morning star is the morning star' differs from
> that expressed by 'the morning star is the evening star.'"

Core move: meaning (Sinn) and reference (Bedeutung) come apart; identity
statements can be informative because a term carries more than what it
points at.
